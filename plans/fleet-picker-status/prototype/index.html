Status words: Claude reports needs you, working, failed, stopped, crashed and done. pir's words are taken
  from its own dashboard (asking you, your go, ready to merge, planning, testing, building, …). A done or finished item
  shows how long ago it finished and disappears after an hour. Failed, stopped and crashed stay until you dismiss them
  in the program itself. Colours here are what the terminal draws: yellow = waiting for you, red = failed or crashed,
  dim = finished. Nothing in this mock is wired to real data.
