What the tags mean

NEW

PR opened within the last 24 hours. The one that just landed and you haven't seen yet.

ACTIVE

Several comments in the last 24 hours. A live discussion is happening right now.

STALE

Proposed. No activity in over 14 days — going cold, may need a nudge or a close.

Age reads 5h under a day, 3d under a week, then a date like Aug 20.

The button reaction (proposal #1)

  • 1Rest — a quiet outline, primary in cyan, Open muted.
  • 2Hover — the button fills faintly and brightens, so the pointer target is unmistakable.
  • 3Press — it inverts for a beat — solid fill, text knocked out — confirming the click registered before the agent spawns.

Try it on the rows above. This is what a terminal pane can plausibly do; whether hover works on the unfocused pane is a technical question the plan will probe first.

Something useful I'd add — your call

A [STALE] tag for a PR with no activity in over two weeks. It completes the activity story the other two tags start: NEW just arrived, ACTIVE is busy now, STALE has gone quiet and is easy to forget. It costs nothing extra — it reads the same update time the age already uses.

Toggle it on and off above to see it in place. Accept, reject, or change the threshold — I won't build it unless you say so.
